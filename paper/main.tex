\begin{abstract}
Current scaling paradigms in Large Language Models (LLMs) rely heavily on massive GPU clusters and non-consensual web scraping. In this paper, we introduce \textbf{ONIWA} (Organic Non-datacenter Intelligence Without Abuse), a project dedicated to cultivating autonomous language intelligence implemented purely in Rust, operating entirely on low-power edge hardware (such as Raspberry Pi 4). ONIWA achieves 100\% provenance and auditability through an immutable ledger index, tracking every training token and weight checksum, while rejecting opaque dependencies. We present the architectural design, thermal-aware edge training dynamics, and empirical evaluation of sustainable, transparent machine learning.
\end{abstract}
